CHAPTER 01 · A LETTER FOR YOU

To the person I still choose.

Hi, baby. There are things I don't always know how to say out loud, so I thought I'd put some of them here. I made this so you could have something to look back on. I hope these pages make you feel a little of the love and appreciation I have for you.

I may not be perfect, and our relationship isn't perfect either, but I'm thankful that I get to share these moments with you.

CHAPTER 02 · OUR JOURNEY

We've had our moments.

I know things haven't always been easy between us. We have misunderstandings, tampo, arguments, and moments when we don't understand each other.

Sometimes, I overthink. Sometimes, I say things without thinking about how they might make you feel. And sometimes, I fail to explain what I really mean.

I'm sorry for the times I've hurt you, especially when all you wanted was to feel understood. I know saying sorry isn't enough if I don't learn from my mistakes.

CHAPTER 03 · GROWING TOGETHER

I'm still learning.

I want us to become better, not just as a couple but also as individuals. I want to learn how to listen more, understand you better, and be more careful with my words.

Dili man ta perfect, and we won't always agree on everything. But I hope we can choose to understand each other instead of letting misunderstandings become bigger.

I want us to feel safe sharing our thoughts, even when the conversation is difficult. We should be on the same team, not against each other.

CHAPTER 04 · THANK YOU

Thank you for staying.

Thank you for the little things you do, for your time, your patience, and the moments when you make me feel appreciated.

Thank you for being part of my life. From the simple conversations to the memories we've made, I appreciate all of them.

One year of knowing you and eleven months of being together may sound like numbers, but for me, they hold so many memories, lessons, and reasons to be thankful.

CHAPTER 05 · HOW IT STARTED

It started with a simple conversation.

Go back to the days when we first talked to each other. Honestly, at first, wala lang talaga to. I didn't expect anything special.

But the more we talked, the more I found myself getting interested in you. I don't know exactly why, but I really wanted to know you more.

I wanted to understand your stories, your thoughts, and the little things about you. I wanted to know the person behind those conversations.

CHAPTER 06 · WHY I ADMIRE YOU

It's the little things.

I didn't fall in love just because of your appearance. It was because of how you treated me, your sweetness, and how diligent you are with your academics.

I admire how you keep doing what you can, bisag kapoy na, you still try to move forward. I see that effort, and it means so much to me.

Your service, your quality time, and even the simplest things you do give me butterflies. Maybe you don't notice it, but those little moments are some of the things I appreciate most.

I like hearing about your day, your random stories, and even the things you think are not that important. I enjoy knowing what's on your mind. Bisan simple nga update lang or random nga topic, it makes me happy nga you choose to share those things with me.

CHAPTER 07 · I'M SORRY

You deserve to be treated right.

I also want to say sorry for the times I made you overthink and for the moments I didn't treat you the way you wanted and deserved to be treated.

Sorry sa akong behavior sometimes, especially sa mga moments nga pati ako wala na kasabot sa akong kaugalingon. I know that doesn't excuse the way I act.

I don't want you to keep questioning your worth because of my actions. You deserve reassurance, understanding, respect, and consistency.

I'm still learning, and I hope my actions will show that I'm serious about becoming better. I don't want my apologies to be just words. I want to learn from them.

CHAPTER 08 · A REMINDER FOR YOU

Please don't be too hard on yourself.

Always remember to take pahuway when you're tired. You don't have to force yourself to do everything at once. Rest is okay, and taking things slowly doesn't mean you're falling behind.

Please don't let negative words make you question who you are. You're more than enough just as you are right now. Dili man pirmi hayag ang atong days, but that doesn't mean we won't have brighter days again.

Maybe not today, maybe not tomorrow, but someday you'll have another chance to shine. For now, focus on your goals and dreams. Keep going at your own pace.

I, your friends, and your family are proud of you. And most importantly, Allah knows every effort you make, even the ones that other people don't see.

CHAPTER 09 · MY FINAL WORDS

And my choice is still you.

I can't promise that I'll never make mistakes, but I can promise that I'll keep trying to become a better person and a better partner.

I want more memories with you, more random conversations, more laughter, and more little moments that we can look back on someday.

I'm thankful for you, for us, and for everything we've learned along the way.

I still choose you, and I still choose us. Not because we're perfect, but because I believe we can keep learning and growing together. And if someday we look back at this notebook, I hope we'll smile knowing how much we've grown since the day I made this. From strangers to people who now share memories, stories, and a special place in each other's lives. I love you.
